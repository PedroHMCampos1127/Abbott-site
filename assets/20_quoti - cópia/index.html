Frequently asked questions

Is Quoti available in French?

Yes. Quoti works in English and French, including your booking page and the texts your clients receive. It was built in Quebec by the owner of a window cleaning company.

Which businesses is Quoti built for?

Owner-operators and small crews in window cleaning, pressure washing and soft washing, gutter cleaning, trash bin cleaning and mobile car detailing. Quoti works best for services you can price from a few questions, without a site visit.

Do I need a website to use Quoti?

No. Your Quoti booking page can be your website: add the link to your Google Business Profile, Facebook page, flyers and truck. If you already have a website, add the booking page to it instead.

What happens when a lead texts me instead of booking online?

Every text, email and job update with a client lands in one conversation in Quoti. Quoti's AI uses your prices, open times and past jobs to reply for you or draft the answer for your approval, and it flags leads who are waiting on you or haven't booked yet.

Can customers get my price without calling me?

Yes. You set your prices once: up to 3 packages per service, add-ons, and the questions that change the price, like the number of floors or windows. Customers see their exact price on your booking page, day or night, so you stop texting prices one lead at a time.

Can I manage my schedule, quotes and invoices in one place?

Yes. Quoti runs the whole job in one app: request, quote, scheduled visit, invoice and payment. Your day shows on a map, clients get "on my way" texts, and a client hub lets them see their visits, pay and book again.

How does Quoti book jobs on autopilot?

You share one booking link on Google, Facebook, Instagram, Marketplace or your website. Leads answer a few questions about their home, see your price right away and pick a time from your real availability. The job lands on your calendar with the address and details, and Quoti sends the confirmation and reminders for you.

How do I get paid?

Turn a finished job into an invoice in seconds and send it by text or email. Clients pay by card online, and the money is paid out to your bank account through Stripe. You can also record cash and check payments and see what is still unpaid at a glance.
